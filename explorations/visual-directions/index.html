NORTHSTAR DSOne visual direction, two ways to get there

The docs look like the dashboards.

Every page becomes one bento board. Section color fills the lead tile, charts sit on solid color, and the landing glow runs on every page as tiles arrive.

  • Loudest. Closest to Campaign command and the film.
  • Charts change ink by tile: white on blue, dark on teal, amber, green.
  • Risk: color means "section" and "data" at once, so status colors need guarding.

Color only where something is live.

The same boards on black with hairlines. Each tile carries one key color as a rail and as its data color. Glow is kept for one thing: the tile that is live, hovered or focused.

  • Calmer. The design system's own styling stays untouched.
  • One chart palette everywhere, so no chart can look out of place.
  • Risk: less of the bold look from the layouts.
