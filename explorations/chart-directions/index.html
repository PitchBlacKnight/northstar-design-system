What is the same in both

Open Sans and JetBrains Mono, the existing palette, 11px axis text in chart-label-text, one series order (teal, blue, purple, violet), status colors kept for status, and a single source in js/viz.js.

What a pick changes

Bar radius, stroke and cap tokens, how tile surfaces are assigned, the style-lint rules, the chart sets in Figma on the next sync, and the visual baselines.

Not shown

Light mode and high contrast. Ink on a color block has to hold 4.5:1 in both modes, so the block colors need a light-mode pass.
